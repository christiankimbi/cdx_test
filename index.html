OUR FLAGSHIP RADIO SHOW: INFO TRAFIC

Our first popular radio content production, Info Trafic, has earned a strong and loyal audience base in Kinshasa. The program:

  • Delivers real-time traffic updates and suggests alternative routes
  • Highlights major traffic issues affecting urban mobility
  • Educates listeners on road safety, etiquette, and regulations

Info Trafic is a trusted voice in daily commutes and a key public education platform
